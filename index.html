Daily Music

Founder and developerJune 2023 – March 2026

Daily Music is a Discord app that recommends music, with quizzes and general knowledge around it. I built it alone, from the code to the community, and it ended up on more than 2,500 servers, with over 445,000 users.

  • I wrote all of it: the app, the REST API, the website, the dashboard, and even a Bluesky account that posts on its own.
  • There was a free version and a premium one, and some of the free users upgraded to premium.
  • Tens of thousands of people came without any ads, through Google, Discord app listing sites and social media.
  • I was also the one answering users, free and premium, and moderating the community.
